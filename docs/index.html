⇄

STEER

AD / ←→ slide across the tube. WS / ↑↓ change altitude. Arrow keys use analog fly-to steering.

✦

FIRE

SPACE or CLICK fires full auto. Drones shatter into score. Collect ARC CELLS to upgrade to twin, spread and lance fire.

⟁

OVERDRIVE

SHIFT drains the boost bar for triple speed and doubled score. Near-misses, combos and pickups recharge it. Never let it sit full.

⬡

SURVIVE

Barriers cost hull. REPAIR cells restore it, SHIELD absorbs one hit, QUANTUM slows time. Gates open between sectors.

☠

DRONES & WARDENS

A red glow means a drone is charging. Destroy it before it fires. At the end of a sector a WARDEN guards the gate — break its armour, then fly the gap.

⚡

COMBOS

Chain kills and pickups without taking a hit to grow the multiplier up to ×8. Hull damage resets the chain. Bank it before the next barrier field.
